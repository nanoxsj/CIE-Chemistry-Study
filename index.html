23 Chemical energetics

  • 23.1 Lattice energy & Born–Haber cycles：晶格能、原子化焓、电子亲和能、Born–Haber 循环与计算
  • 23.2 Enthalpies of solution & hydration：溶解焓、水合焓、能量循环与影响水合焓的因素

  • 23.4 Gibbs free energy change：\(\Delta G^\ominus=\Delta H^\ominus-T\Delta S^\ominus\)、反应可行性判断与温度影响

24 Electrochemistry

  • 24.1 Electrolysis：Faraday、\(Q=It\)、\(F=Le\)、electroplating 与 Avogadro constant calculations
  • 24.2 Standard electrode potentials, standard cell potentials and the Nernst equation：SHE、half-cells、cell diagrams、salt bridge、\(E^\ominus_{\mathrm{cell}}\)、Nernst equation、Gibbs energy 与氧化还原可行性

25 Equilibria

  • 25.1 Acids and bases：Brønsted–Lowry acids and bases、conjugate pairs、pH、\(K_a\)、p\(K_a\)、\(K_w\)、strong / weak acids、buffers、blood bicarbonate buffer、\(K_{sp}\) 与 common-ion effect
  • 25.2 Partition coefficients：\(K_{pc}\) 定义、计算、相对溶解度与 solute / solvent polarity

26 Reaction kinetics

  • 26.1 Simple rate equations, orders of reaction and rate constants：rate equation、orders、initial rates、graphs、half-life、\(k\)、mechanisms、rate-determining steps、Arrhenius relationship
  • 26.2 Homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysts (AL)：adsorption、bond weakening、desorption、Haber process、catalytic converters、Fe\(^{2+}\)/Fe\(^{3+}\) cycle 与 nitrogen oxides catalysis

27 Group 2

  • 27.1 Similarities and trends in the properties of the Group 2 metals, magnesium to barium, and their compounds (AL)：ionic radius、charge density、polarisation、nitrate/carbonate thermal stability、water reactions、hydroxide/sulfate solubility、lattice energy、hydration enthalpy 与 enthalpy of solution

28 Chemistry of transition elements

  • 28.1 General physical and chemical properties：transition element 定义、电子排布、variable oxidation states、complex formation、colour 和 catalysis
  • 28.2 General characteristic chemical properties：ligands、coordination number、complex geometries、ligand exchange 与 redox catalysis
  • 28.3 Colour of complexes：degenerate / non-degenerate d orbitals、octahedral / tetrahedral splitting、visible-light absorption 与 complementary colours
  • 28.4 Stereoisomerism：cis–trans geometrical isomerism 与 optical isomerism
  • 28.5 Stability constants：\(K_{\mathrm{stab}}\) 表达式、ligand exchange 和稳定性比较

29 An introduction to A Level organic chemistry

  • 29.1 Formulas, Functional Groups - the Naming of Organic Compounds (AL)：functional groups、general / structural / displayed / skeletal formula、脂肪族与芳香族化合物的系统命名

  • 29.3 Shapes of Aromatic Organic Molecules; σ - π Bonds：benzene 的平面结构、\(sp^2\) hybridisation、\(\sigma\) bonds 与 delocalised \(\pi\) system
  • 29.4 Isomerism Optical：chiral centre、enantiomers、plane-polarised light、racemic mixture 与 chiral catalysts 在药物合成中的意义

30 Hydrocarbons

31 Halogen compounds

  • 31.1 Halogen compounds：benzene 与 methylbenzene 的 halogenation、electrophilic substitution、2,4-directing effects、side-chain free-radical substitution、halogenoalkanes 与 halogenoarenes 的 nucleophilic substitution reactivity、C–X bond 的 partial double-bond character 与 aromatic synthesis

  • 33.2 Esters：esterification、condensation、hydrolysis、naming 与 reversible equilibrium

34 Nitrogen compounds

  • 34.2 Phenylamine and azo compounds：nitrobenzene reduction、bromination、diazonium salts、azo coupling 与 azo dyes
  • 34.3 Amides：formation、acid/alkaline hydrolysis、LiAlH₄ reduction 与 amides 的 weak basicity
